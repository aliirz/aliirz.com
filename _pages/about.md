I'm Ali. I write software, and I write about the software I write.

I live in Istanbul. I take on fractional CTO work. I'm a co-founder at [BoostPanda](https://boostpanda.ai/), where we build Pandy — the harness around a model, so it can do actual work. A prompt is not a program.

Most of what I figure out ends up here. Lately that's been agent fleets, memory that survives a session, and why the cheap harness for one model is expensive for another. I spent two years prompting models the hard way, then wrote [Harmless DSPy](https://harmlessdspy.com/) about programming them instead.

I also care about systems that don't ask you to trust them on faith. [phntm.sh](https://phntm.sh/) is a file-sharing tool I built so the file encrypts in your browser and the key never touches the server. Trust me is not an architecture.

Before Istanbul I spent years in civic tech, mostly with [Code for Pakistan](https://codeforpakistan.org/), on identity for government services, legal literacy, and whether public websites actually stayed up. That chapter ended in December 2025. The interest didn't.

I refactor a lot and make lame puns.
